# Final Project — AI-Assisted ANN Concept Demonstration

## Overview
Each group selects one concept from Artificial Neural Networks and produces a clear, interactive, and visually engaging explanation of it — using AI tools as assistants (not substitutes) throughout the process.

The goal is not just to explain a concept accurately, but to demonstrate the ability to communicate and teach complex ideas effectively, with AI-assisted tooling.

## Deliverables

### 1. Presentation (PPT)
A visually clear and structured slide deck explaining the concept, including:
- Intuitive diagrams and worked examples
- Structured flow from motivation → mechanism → application
- Limitations and failure cases
- Brief comparison with a related concept

### 2. Simulation / Interactive Demo / Video
A concise practical demonstration of the concept in action. This may be:
- An interactive visualization (e.g., web-based demo)
- An animated explanation video
- A runnable simulation with observable behaviour

### 3. MCQ Bank
20 multiple-choice questions covering the main aspects of the concept, with correct answers identified. Questions range from definitional to application-level.

### 4. Report
Documents the full process:
- Aim of the work
- Block diagram of steps followed
- AI tools used and how they were used (with honest documentation)
- Challenges encountered and how they were resolved
- Final conclusions
